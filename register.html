Seller registration

Complete the form below to create a seller account. Fields marked * are required. All six fields are validated on the client side with a regular expression, and nothing is submitted until every one of them passes.

Personal details

Letters and spaces only, for example Zhang San

A mainland China mobile number: 11 digits starting with 1

Letters, digits and spaces only. Punctuation is not accepted.

Must contain exactly one @ and end with .com or .cn

Account details

At least 6 characters, letters or digits only

At least 6 characters, letters or digits only

Teaching note: this site is a front-end demonstration only, so passwords are stored as plain text in the browser localStorage. A real system must hash and salt them on the server, which is covered in Phase B.

Validation rules at a glance

Full name
Letters and spaces only
Address
Letters, digits and spaces only
Phone number
11 mainland China digits, starting 1 with a second digit of 3 to 9
Email
Exactly one @, ending in .com or .cn
Username
At least 6 letters or digits
Password
At least 6 letters or digits

What to do after registering

  1. Sign in with the username and password you just chose
  2. Open the post a car page, fill in the car details and upload a photograph
  3. Check the search page to confirm your car can now be found

About AutoLink

An online car sales platform for private sellers. Sellers publish their own listings and set their own prices; buyers can search and view car details without registering.
