Seller centre

This page gathers every entry point a seller needs to manage car adverts. The three actions required by the specification — registration, sign-in and posting a car — are always shown. Once you are signed in, your account details and the cars you have posted are shown as well.

Seller actions

The three routes are independent of each other, and each one leads back to this page.

1

Seller registration

Register before using the site for the first time. A regular expression checks each of the six fields — name, address, phone number, email, username and password — and the account is only created once all six pass.

Register
2

Seller sign-in

Sellers who already have an account sign in here. The session is kept in this browser, so refreshing the page or moving to another page will not sign you out.

Sign in
3

Post a car

Once signed in, enter the colour, model, year, location and price and upload a photograph. The car appears in the search results as soon as you submit it.

Post a car
