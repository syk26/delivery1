About AutoLink

What we do

AutoLink was founded in 2026 as an online platform focused on private used car sales. Unlike a traditional dealership, we let sellers publish their own car details and set their own prices. The platform simply puts each car in front of the buyers who are looking for it.

We believe transparent information reduces friction in a sale. Every listing carries a photograph, colour, year, location and price, so a buyer already holds the information they need before they ever contact the seller.

Main features

  • Seller registration with client-side regular expression validation on all six fields
  • Seller sign-in with a session that survives a page refresh
  • Post a car: colour, model, year, location, price and photograph
  • Search by model and year, with substring matching
  • Car detail page with a large photograph, full specifications and seller contact details
  • Responsive layout for desktop, laptop and tablet screens
